Solvophobic Stiffening

This simulation demonstrates a breakthrough TPE material that gets stronger when exposed to oil.

How it works: The polymer chains contain special supramolecular motifs (pink nodes). These nodes hate oil.

When oil enters the system, the nodes rush to connect with each other to hide from the oil. This "hugging" effect tightens the molecular mesh, increasing stiffness.
