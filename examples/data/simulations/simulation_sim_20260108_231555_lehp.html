Hybrid B: Induction-Vitrimer

The Concept: Rapidly curing a high-performance skin using magnetic fields.

The resin is filled with tiny Iron Oxide particles. When the Induction Coil passes over, it magnetically excites these particles, generating heat from the inside out. This instantly locks the liquid resin into a solid, recyclable solid.
