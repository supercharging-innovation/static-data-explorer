The "Dry-Soft" Paradox

This material solves the trade-off between softness and stickiness.

1. Softness: The blue gel base absorbs energy (hysteresis), giving it a premium, fleshy feel.

2. Dryness: The white micro-pillars reduce contact area, preventing the "sticky" feeling usually associated with soft gels.
